    8-Week Roadmap

    Internship plan, week by week.

    Week 1 · Research & Problem UnderstandingStudy digital addiction, analyze existing platforms, define key metrics, finalize scope and stack.
    Week 2 · Data Collection & PreparationGather datasets, clean with Pandas, structure into database, run EDA.
    Week 3 · Database & Backend SetupSchema design, backend environment, APIs, storage/retrieval, activity tracking modules.
    Week 4 · Dashboard & VisualizationInteractive dashboards, usage charts, addiction indicators, frontend–API integration.
    Week 5 · AI/ML Model DevelopmentPrepare features, build risk prediction model, train/test, evaluate and optimize.
    Week 6 · Recommendation SystemPersonalized suggestions engine, reminder/notification system, improved interaction.
    Week 7 · Testing & OptimizationSystem tests, bug fixes, performance tuning, edge cases and missing data handling.
    Week 8 · Deployment & Final PresentationCloud deploy, documentation, demo video, final presentation, GitHub submission.
